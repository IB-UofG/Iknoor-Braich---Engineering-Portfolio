About

Hey I'm Iknoor, a third-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Guelph. Currently I'm on the brakes sub-team for Gryphon Racing's Formula SAE team designing components, making drawings and fabricating in shop. I like projects that force me to learn something new, whether that is adapting a PID control loop to hardware it wasn't built for, choosing materials based on performance data instead of convenience or figuring out why a design that worked on paper didn't hold up once it was built. That process of designing, testing, and correcting course is what I try to bring to every project I take on.
